About me

Hi, I'm Sriya.

A public policy practitioner working where policy design meets the realities of the field — and where, I've learned, influence comes not from authority but from alignment.

I work at the intersection of equity, systems design, and human development — most often in public health, the lever I'm most driven to pull.

Structured Problem Solving Stakeholder Engagement Policy Communication Adaptive Leadership MEL & Results Frameworks Implementation Monitoring Indicator Design Excel · Basic R

Languages — English & Hindi (expert) · Marathi · Konkani
Beyond work — Badminton, Swimming, Kathak

I pursued a B.A. (Hons.) in Humanities and Social Sciences from Cluster Innovation Centre, University of Delhi — interdisciplinary training in Public Policy, Economics, and Political Science across Miranda House, LSR and SRCC. I then completed an M.A. in Public Administration at Jamia Millia Islamia to deepen my understanding of governance and policymaking.

My journey in the social sector began as a Consultant at the Rajya Sabha, where I gained exposure to parliamentary functioning. I went on to join the Centre for Public Policy and Governance as a Research Associate in the public health vertical, leading flagship convenings and CSR fundraising.

Increasingly drawn to hands-on public administration and the last-mile delivery of public systems, I transitioned into public service through the PPiA Praxis Residency Program. Embedded within district administration, I've come to see that the hardest problems are rarely technical — they're adaptive, demanding behavioural and systemic change.

That belief shapes how I work: start with diagnosis, validate assumptions on the ground, and design solutions that strengthen systems — not just inputs.
